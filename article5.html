AWS Review Analysis and Customer Insights

Positive review analysis

Review collection

I collected positive reviews about AWS from Google Maps and online platforms, focusing on customer experiences related to scalability, reliability, and performance. I selected reviews that highlighted strengths in cloud services, uptime, and flexibility.

Analyzing positive review

First analysis

I analyzed positive reviews using ChatGPT to identify key themes and customer satisfaction drivers.

Prompt Used:
“Analyze the following positive AWS customer reviews and identify key themes, strengths, and reasons for satisfaction.”

Result:
Customers consistently praised AWS for high scalability, reliability, and wide range of services.
Strong satisfaction with uptime and performance.
Flexibility and global infrastructure were frequently mentioned as key advantages.
Developers appreciated integration with DevOps tools.

Second analysis (further analysis – word association)

I conducted a word association analysis to identify commonly repeated keywords in positive reviews.

Prompt Used:
“Extract and group the most common words and phrases from positive AWS reviews.”

Result:
Common keywords: scalable, reliable, fast, flexible, global, powerful, secure
These words highlight AWS’s positioning as a high-performance and enterprise-ready platform.

Recommendation

I asked ChatGPT to generate marketing recommendations based on positive review insights.

Prompt Used:
“Based on positive AWS reviews, suggest marketing strategies to reinforce these strengths.”

Result:
Emphasize scalability and reliability in marketing campaigns.
Use customer success stories and case studies.
Highlight global infrastructure and uptime guarantees.
Promote DevOps integrations and performance benefits.

Negative review analysis

Review collection

I collected negative reviews focusing on customer complaints related to pricing, complexity, and support experience. These reviews provided insight into pain points and user frustrations.

Analyzing negative review

First analysis

I analyzed negative reviews using ChatGPT to identify recurring issues and dissatisfaction factors.

Prompt Used:
“Analyze the following negative AWS reviews and identify common complaints and user frustrations.”

Result:
Users frequently complained about unpredictable pricing.
The platform was described as complex and difficult for beginners.
Customer support response times were mentioned as inconsistent.
Some users experienced difficulty navigating the interface.

Second analysis (further analysis – word association)

I performed a word association analysis to identify repeated negative sentiment keywords.

Prompt Used:
“Extract common negative keywords and themes from AWS customer complaints.”

Result:
Common keywords: expensive, complex, confusing, difficult, unclear pricing
These words highlight key barriers to adoption and user experience challenges.

Recommendation

I asked ChatGPT to generate strategic recommendations based on negative feedback insights.

Prompt Used:
“Based on negative AWS reviews, suggest improvements and marketing strategies to address these issues.”

Result:
Improve pricing transparency and cost estimation tools.
Simplify onboarding and user interface for beginners.
Create more educational content and tutorials.
Enhance customer support responsiveness.
Promote pricing calculators and cost control tools more clearly.
