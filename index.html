David Chen's Oralce Experience

I've been using Oralce for years. When you're running mission critical systems for a fortuen 500 company like I am, can't afford to have downtime. Oracle's proven track record and enterprise support give me the confidence I need to get a good nights rest. Yes, we may pay a premium fof the services and yes the licesning part may be a little complex. But when our database is process $500 million transactions an hour, that reliability is worth every penny. Our customers don't care if AWS might be a little cheaper. They care that thier account balances are accurate.

Sara Thompson on her choice of Salesforce

I need a platform that doesn't just hold on to customer data for me, I need one that gives me the actionable instightsthat help my team close deals and do it efficiently.
