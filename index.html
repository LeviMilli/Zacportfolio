Welcome to TheBusinessBlog

“As part of my Digital Marketing course, I built this website to apply the concepts and tools I am learning in class to real-world contexts. Throughout the semester, I will connect this site with key digital tools, such as Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Search Console (GSC), and email automation, and use them to understand how online content performs and how users interact with a website. In this course, I am learning the foundational skills of digital marketing: search engine optimization (SEO), content marketing, social media strategy, and analytics. I am also practicing how to use AI tools for content creation, keyword research, image generation, and data-driven decision-making. The goal is not only to understand digital marketing in theory but to apply it in a practical and measurable way. To make the learning meaningful, I selected two companies that I am personally interested in. Throughout the semester, I will create content and analyses related to these companies. This project allows me to demonstrate my skills, contribute insights that may be useful for the companies
