Digital Marketing Course Reflection & Portfolio Summary

Course Overview

This course provided a comprehensive introduction to digital marketing, combining both theoretical knowledge and hands-on projects. The structure focused on understanding digital environments, applying marketing strategies, and using real tools such as Google Analytics, SEO techniques, and AI platforms.

Theoretical Knowledge Gained

I developed a strong understanding of digital marketing concepts including consumer behavior, search engine optimization (SEO), email marketing, and social media marketing. I also learned how businesses position themselves online and how digital channels influence purchasing decisions.

Practical Project Summary

Throughout the course, I built my own website, created customer personas, conducted SEO keyword research, and developed blog content using AI tools. I also worked on real-world case analysis for companies such as AWS and Salesforce.

Key Skills Acquired

I gained practical skills in website development, content creation, SEO strategy, Google Analytics (GA4), and Google Search Console. I also learned how to analyze customer data and improve marketing performance based on insights.

AI Tool Utilization Experience

I used ChatGPT extensively for generating customer personas, keyword research, blog writing, and marketing strategies. I also used Leonardo.AI to create visual content. These tools significantly improved efficiency and content quality.

Data Analysis Capabilities

I developed the ability to analyze customer reviews and website analytics using GA4. I learned how to interpret engagement metrics, session data, and user behavior to improve digital marketing strategies.

Real Company Analysis Experience

I conducted marketing analysis for AWS and Salesforce, identifying strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities based on customer data and reviews. I also proposed actionable marketing strategies for both companies.

Social Media Utilization

I used LinkedIn to share blog posts and engage with a professional audience. This helped me build my network and understand how content performs in real-world marketing environments.

Key Achievements and Insights

One of my biggest achievements was successfully creating a fully functional marketing website and publishing multiple blog posts. I also realized how important data-driven decision-making is in digital marketing.

Future Application Plans

I plan to continue using these skills to build and manage marketing campaigns, improve SEO performance, and analyze customer data to optimize business outcomes.

Contribution Plans for Target Companies

Based on my experience, I can contribute to companies by improving digital marketing strategies, optimizing SEO performance, creating engaging content, and analyzing customer data to drive growth.

Personal Growth
